Authentication system in Django

  • Category: Django
  • Project: Django course for beginners
  • Published: 2022-06-16
  • Platform: Django Workshop

In this lesson we will show you how to let users log in to your site with their accounts and how to control what they can do and see based on whether they are registered and what permissions they have. As part of this demo, we will extend the local library website by adding login and logout pages, as well as user pages and personal pages for viewing books that have been borrowed.
